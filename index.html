02 / Research roadmap

From first principles to training recipes.

I specialize in large language model pre-training, with a focus on optimization, training dynamics and stability, scaling laws, and model merging. My work follows a consistent loop: identify structure in large-scale training, explain it through optimization and spectral dynamics, then turn the mechanism into a robust recipe that survives scale.

  1. 01

    Read the dynamics

    Find the quantities that actually organize training: effective learning rate, sharpness, singular spectra, and checkpoint trajectories.

    Signal → structure
  2. 02

    Design the geometry

    Use subspace structure to reason about Muon, Shampoo, momentum, and model merging—not as isolated tricks, but as geometric operators.

    Structure → algorithm
  3. 03

    Make it scale

    Translate controlled experiments into stable schedules, optimizer choices, and overtraining ladders for production pre-training.

    Algorithm → recipe

ICML 2026Model merging

Extra-Merge: Tracing the Rank-1 Subspace of Model Merging in Language Model Pre-Training

Wenjie Zhou, Bohan Wang, Hongtao Zhang, Chenxi Jia, Wei Chen, Xueqi Cheng

Shows that late-stage merged checkpoints collapse onto a stable, nearly rank-1 flow even when raw updates oscillate. Extra-Merge extrapolates along that direction—improving models without another gradient step across GPT-2, LLaMA, Pythia-12B, and Muon settings.

ICML 2026Spectral dynamics

The Stability of Singular Distribution: A Spectral Perspective on the Two-Phase Dynamics of Language Model Pre-training

Hongtao Zhang*, Wenjie Zhou*, Chenxi Jia, Wei Chen, Xueqi Cheng

Identifies Stability of Singular Distribution (SoSD): normalized singular spectra settle early, at the same point that loss enters its slow phase. The effect persists across model families, schedules, weight decay, and AdamW/Muon, giving a spectral explanation of two-phase training.

EMNLP 2025 · OralOptimization

BSFA: Leveraging the Subspace Dichotomy to Accelerate Neural Network Training

Wenjie Zhou*, Bohan Wang*, Wei Chen, Xueqi Cheng

Separates updates into a high-curvature dominant subspace and a descent-efficient bulk subspace, then suppresses the former while amplifying the latter. A block-wise, history-based implementation acts as a scalable optimizer plug-in and accelerates LLaMA pre-training.

ICML 2026 · HiLDMuon

When and Why Grouping Attention Heads Accelerates Muon Optimization

Hongtao Zhang*, Wenjie Zhou*, Wei Chen, Xueqi Cheng

Explains why Muon’s best whitening granularity changes during training through a trade-off between group-wise whitening gain and grouping-induced norm cost. Group Muon makes head grouping an optimizer control and outperforms both full-QKV and fully head-wise variants.

How Bregman Divergences Shape Shampoo

Bing Liu, Wenjie Zhou, Chengcheng Zhao, Hongtao Zhang, Boao Kong, Felix Dangel, Wu Lin

Builds a unified Bregman-divergence view of Shampoo’s Kronecker approximation. It shows how each divergence redistributes spectral error and compensates differently for finite-sample bias, then connects those choices to GPT-2 pre-training behavior.

ICML 2026 · HiLDKronecker geometry

Rethinking Bregman Divergences in Kronecker-Factored Optimizers

Bing Liu*, Wenjie Zhou*, Chengcheng Zhao

Characterizes how Frobenius, von Neumann, and LogDet divergences allocate unavoidable Kronecker error across the covariance spectrum. The analysis motivates subspace-aware BregTop using the observed alignment between Hessian and covariance top eigenspaces.
